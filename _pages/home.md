## What We Do

We host regular online seminars where members present, discuss, and debate ideas about consciousness from every disciplinary angle — psychology, physics, neuroscience, philosophy, and beyond. Our discussions are transcribed, and most are shared publicly after member review. We also develop collaborative open-access projects (courses, workshops, and outreach) under the CO-LAB umbrella.

Our permanent base is at **Zhejiang University** in Hangzhou, where local members gather in person for our online sessions. Everyone else joins by video conference, from anywhere in the world.

---

## Why CO-LAB?

Most academic platforms run on grants. **We run on curiosity.**  
Most consciousness discussion groups sit in Europe or North America. **We are anchored in Hangzhou, open to the world.**  
Most discussions stay behind closed doors. **Ours are transcribed and shared publicly.**

If you think consciousness deserves to be discussed across disciplines, without hierarchy, and in the open — **you belong here.**
