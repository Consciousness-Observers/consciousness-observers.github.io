All CO-LAB discussions take place via video conference. Local members in Hangzhou gather at Zhejiang University; everyone else joins remotely. Every session is recorded for transcription purposes only — we publish text transcripts after review and approval by all participants. **Original video recordings are never made public.**

We believe this strikes the right balance: ideas circulate freely, but participants can explore tentative thoughts without permanent exposure.
