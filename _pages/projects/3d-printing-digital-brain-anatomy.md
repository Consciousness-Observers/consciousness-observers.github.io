A hands-on workshop series that combines 3D printing technology with digital neuroanatomy tools to make brain structure tangible and accessible. Participants will learn to work with open-source neuroimaging data, segment brain regions of interest, and produce physical 3D-printed models for teaching, outreach, and research communication.

Full workshop syllabus, session schedule, and materials will be published here soon. [Contact the project lead](mailto:psychwangzihao@zju.edu.cn?subject=3D%20Printing%20and%20Digital%20Brain%20Anatomy) if you'd like to be notified when details are available.
