## 想象 · Imagine

> If "imagination" is not idle fantasy but a **sixth sense** we have not yet fully decoded — then, when that sense goes missing, how astonishingly diverse can human consciousness become?

---

## About the Project

**Who I am, what I am searching for.**

As a researcher who studies *imagination as a form of perception*, I keep returning to one question: **have we become too dependent on "images" and "voices" to define the inner world?**

In mainstream cognition, imagination is usually treated as an accessory of vision or hearing. But my research points to imagination as an **independent and fundamental channel of perception**. To test this, I turn to people with deeply unusual inner lives — **aphantasics**, who cannot form mental images, and **those without an inner voice**, who cannot hear narrative in their minds.

These individuals are not "broken" versions of consciousness. On the contrary, they are the **X-rays** of our understanding of consciousness. By comparing their cognitive patterns with those of typical individuals, we can glimpse the fault lines in the underlying architecture of conscious experience.

This project braids together **empirical neuroscience**, **phenomenological descriptions of experience**, and the **sensory expression of contemporary art**. Science provides the evidence, philosophy provides the depth, and art provides the bridge that lets ordinary people *feel* what it is like.

---

## The Approach — Three Pillars

The research is not confined to the laboratory; it unfolds along three parallel lines:

1. **🧠 The testimony of science — neuroscience.** Using EEG and fMRI, capture the neural substrates of a brain *when it cannot imagine*, in search of objective biomarkers of imaginative activity.
2. **📜 The suspension of philosophy — phenomenology.** Drawing on Husserl and Merleau-Ponty, guide participants through rigorous first-person descriptions of experience — suspending the natural attitude and returning to the living scene of conscious experience.
3. **🎨 The translation of art — perceptual transcendence.** Through interactive installations and media experiments, translate the experience of *absence* into light, space, and interaction that "normal perceivers" can touch — a collective escape from ordinary perception.

## The Exhibition Project

I am working toward a bold plan: in **Hangzhou**, China's innovation capital, in collaboration with the **School of Intermedia Art, China Academy of Art** and **Zhejiang University (College of AI / Art & Archaeology Museum)**, to stage a cutting-edge art exhibition on **consciousness, missing imagination, and phenomenological experience**.

**What the exhibition will do:**

- **Transcend perception.** Following the spirit of Tsinghua University's *Another Sense* exhibition, we will let audiences — in dark rooms and before interactive installations — be temporarily *deprived* or *confused* in their vision and hearing, so they can feel the everyday world of aphantasics and of those without an inner voice.
- **Public science.** Turn rigorous psychological and neuroscientific research into a visual narrative with real tension, so that every ordinary visitor can feel the science of consciousness in a visceral way.
- **Find companions.** The exhibition will include a strictly anonymous psychophysical screening channel, precisely recruiting aphantasics and people without an inner voice for our laboratory.
