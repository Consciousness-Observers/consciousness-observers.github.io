## Who Can Join

If you are affiliated with a university or research institute and care deeply about consciousness, you are welcome here. We ask two things:

1. **You have an institutional academic affiliation.** You may be a student at any level, a postdoc, a research assistant, or an independent researcher — as long as your work is grounded in an academic institution.
2. **You come to learn, not to fundraise.** CO-LAB has no funding and distributes none. We are a purely intellectual community.

We do not charge fees, award grants, or pay salaries. We meet because we find the question of consciousness worth our time.

---

## How to Join

Admission is community-governed and proceeds in three steps:

**Step 1 — Observation.** An existing member invites you to attend 2–3 sessions as an observer. You are welcome to participate in discussions during this period.

**Step 2 — Recommendation.** At least one member writes a brief recommendation for your membership.

**Step 3 — Vote.** All current members vote on your admission. Voting is mandatory — the process concludes only after every member has cast their vote. Admission requires a majority approval.

---

## Community Norms

We are united by curiosity, not by credentials. These norms are how we protect that:

- **No hierarchy.** Academic titles and seniority carry no weight in CO-LAB discussions. A first-year undergraduate and a tenured professor are equals here.
- **No academic bullying.** Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, condescension, and gatekeeping are not.
- **Respect every paradigm.** Consciousness can be studied through behavior, brain imaging, physics, philosophy, computation, and more. No single approach is privileged.
- **What is said here stays here.** Raw discussion content (video, unedited transcript, chat logs) is never shared without explicit permission from every participant.
