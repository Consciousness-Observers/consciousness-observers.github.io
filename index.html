Consciousness Observers

CO-LAB · Linking Across Boundaries

A student-led team working on consciousness across physics, psychology, neuroscience, biomedical engineering and philosophy.

We are a handful of students who want to understand consciousness. Between us we cover most of the disciplines the question touches, and most of the programmes the university has built for it. The name says the rest: Consciousness Observers — Linking Across Boundaries.

We work decentralised: every member takes part in decisions and in the chores, and there are no ranks. We read original papers together, argue about them, and turn what survives into something others can use — a workshop, a reading record, this site.

CO-LAB became a resident team of the Future Learning Center, Zhejiang University in 2026. We have desks there, and can use the centre's shared spaces, 3D printers and student network. Somewhere to leave the reading list, the open questions and half-finished workshop material between sessions matters more than it sounds. We remain an independent, cross-school student group — not attached to one department, not run by faculty.

We run a reading group — one concept per session, the original papers behind it, five evenings a term, the records kept as an open book. We run a full-day workshop that goes from evolutionary origins to a 3D-printed brain. Members also present their own work across disciplines, and what is worth keeping is written up in public.

Write to consciousness.observers@outlook.com — to collaborate, sit in on a session, or argue. What we are reading and running goes on our official account.
